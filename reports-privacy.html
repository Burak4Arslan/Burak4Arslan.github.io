Burak Arslan Reports - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Burak Arslan Reports is a private tool that runs on the developer's own computer. It is used only by the developer, Burak Arslan, to read the statistics of his own apps.

What it accesses

  • AdMob (read-only): ad earnings, impressions and ad unit lists of the developer's own AdMob account.
  • Google Cloud Storage (read-only): the Google Play Console statistics reports (installs, ratings, store listing performance) that Google Play exports for the developer's own apps.

It cannot change anything in these accounts.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to analyze the performance of the developer's own apps.
  • Sign-in tokens and downloaded reports stay on the developer's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by this tool, sold or shared with third parties.
  • No data about other people is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
