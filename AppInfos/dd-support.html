Deep Dive – Support

How to play

Hold anywhere on the screen to dive, release to float back up. Slip past fish, jellyfish, nets and the bosses of the deep, and collect pearls on the way. Your depth is your score.

Tips

  • Short taps hold your depth; long holds dive fast.
  • A magnet pulls in nearby pearls; a shield saves you from one hit.
  • Spend pearls in the Shop on upgrades and diver suits.
  • Daily and rank missions in the menu pay extra pearls.
  • The game works offline. Rewarded videos need a connection.

Report a problem

Write to burak4arslan@gmail.com. It helps a lot if you add your device model, its iOS or Android version, the game version (shown under About in the menu) and, if you can, a screenshot.

Ideas and feedback are just as welcome.
